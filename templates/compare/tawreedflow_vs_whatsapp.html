TawreedFlow vs. WhatsApp & Spreadsheets

Why single-property chat workflows collapse as hotel groups scale — and how purpose-built software establishes strict financial controls, automated quote comparison, and full compliance.

Fragmented Approvals

A Chef messages a request at 6 AM; the GM replies with a thumbs-up emoji on WhatsApp; finance never sees the paper trail until an unauthorized invoice arrives at month-end.

Taxonomy & SKU Decay

Property A orders "Fresh Whole Chicken 1kg," Property B logs "Griller Poultry," and Property C uses vendor SKUs. Without a unified item master, volume bargaining is impossible.

Unverified Dock Deliveries

Suppliers deliver partial quantities or substitute inferior items. Paper delivery notes sit in receiving drawers without 3-way matching against approved purchase orders.
