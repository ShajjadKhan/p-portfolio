Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Immediately

Core Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

FastTrack Tools is architected with a fundamental privacy-first principle: your files never leave your device. When you convert JPG to PDF, merge documents, resize images, or calculate financial metrics, all byte manipulations and algorithmic operations occur entirely within your local web browser using JavaScript, WebAssembly, and HTML5 Canvas. We do not store, view, copy, or retain your files on any remote server.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how FastTrack Tools ("we", "us", "our"), developed and maintained by Shajjad Khan (accessible via www.shajjadkhan.com), collects, uses, and protects your information when you access our online utilities suite.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect (Zero File Storage)

Unlike traditional online converter services, FastTrack Tools does NOT upload your documents, images, or input text to a backend cloud database. Specifically:

  • No Document Uploads: Files selected for PDF merging, splitting, rearranging, or image conversion remain strictly in your browser's local RAM.
  • No Document Retention: When you close the browser tab or refresh the page, all temporary memory allocations are instantly purged.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, submit an email address, or provide personal credentials to use all 32 utilities.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

To support free public access and offset the server bandwidth, maintenance, and hosting costs of running FastTrack Tools, we partner with third-party advertising networks, primarily Google AdSense.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on FastTrack Tools.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized or contextual advertisements to users based on their visits to our website and other sites across the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

FastTrack Tools itself does not set tracking or profiling cookies for its utility functions. Third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies and web beacons on our site to measure the effectiveness of their advertisements and personalize advertising content.

5. Analytics and Server Logs

Standard HTTP access logs may record your IP address, browser user-agent, request timestamp, and referring URL strictly for telemetry, abuse prevention, and server reliability. These logs are not associated with any individual files processed within the client-side utilities.

6. Children's Information (COPPA)

FastTrack Tools does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately so we can remove such records.
