1. Executive Summary & Multi-Branch Operational Friction

Multi-branch commercial operations and regional hospitality groups face recurring administrative friction: reconciling attendance logs across biometric time clocks in different physical facilities, managing multi-tier leave approval hierarchies, and calculating error-free monthly payroll with complex deductions and allowances.

2. Hardware Biometric Clock Synchronization

Architected automated background worker services that interface directly with on-premise biometric time clocks (ZKTeco standalone terminals):

  • Automated Check-In Ingestion: Syncs daily employee punch logs over local network sockets into central PostgreSQL storage.
  • Intelligent Shift Matching: Automatically classifies split shifts, overtime hours, grace period late arrivals, and unapproved absences without human intervention.
  • Hierarchical Leave Routing: Multi-tiered approval workflow (Supervisor → Department Head → HR Director) with instant email and mobile notifications.

3. Automated Payroll & Pay Slip Generation

The system compiles month-end attendance records directly into a automated payroll calculation engine. Base salaries, transport allowances, housing stipends, and GOSI deductions are computed with zero spreadsheet leakage. Individual digitally sealed PDF pay slips are generated asynchronously via Celery and dispatched automatically to staff.
