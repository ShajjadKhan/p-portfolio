The Industry Challenge

Hospitality properties in the GCC process millions in monthly food, beverage, engineering, and operational requisitions. Traditional operations relied on chaotic, un-audited WhatsApp chats and fragmented spreadsheets, resulting in lost quotes, procurement leakage, and lack of compliance with financial controls.

The Architectural Solution

TawreedFlow creates a single source of truth across the entire procurement lifecycle. Requisitions flow seamlessly through Department Heads, Purchasing Coordinators, and Financial Directors. Automated RFQs dispatch directly to vetted suppliers, enabling instant quotation comparison with item substitute flags and purchase order delivery verification.

Enterprise Role-Based Access

Engineered with strict tenant isolation supporting multi-property hotel chains: Master Admins, Directors, Purchasing Receivers, Department Heads, and external Supplier Portals (Owners, Admins, Field Drivers).

Centralizes purchase requisitions, multi-tier approval matrices (Chef → Purchasing → GM/Finance), and automated vendor dispatching to streamline hotel engineering and operational procurement across the Gulf region.

QUESTIONS & GOVERNANCE

Enterprise FAQ

Direct answers regarding confidentiality, IP ownership, data residency, and collaboration workflows.

Do you execute mutual NDAs before reviewing proprietary workflows?

Yes, absolutely. Prior to reviewing internal procurement flows, supplier pricing, or existing architecture, we execute a standard bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement. Your operational trade secrets, vendor matrices, and strategic data remain strictly confidential.

Can our applications and databases remain hosted strictly within Saudi Arabia?

Yes. All platforms are engineered for sovereign deployment on certified regional infrastructure (such as local Saudi cloud providers in Riyadh/Jeddah or client on-premise Debian/Ubuntu Linux servers) in full compliance with SDAIA's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and CST data localization mandates.

Who owns the intellectual property and source code upon completion?

You own 100% of the custom application source code, schemas, and intellectual property upon final delivery and settlement. There are no recurring proprietary software licensing fees from me, no vendor lock-in, and full technical handover documentation is included.

Can custom builds integrate with existing ERPs, Oracle Opera, or ZATCA?

Yes. We engineer integration pipelines for ZATCA Phase 2 cryptographic e-invoicing (generating signed UBL 2.1 XML and Phase 2 clearance webhooks currently in active rollout), alongside REST, GraphQL, or database sync pipelines for systems like SAP, Oracle Opera, or custom legacy software.

What working hours do you keep, and how do we communicate during a build?

I operate on Arabia Standard Time (AST / UTC+3), based in Riyadh. For active engagements, communication is conducted via dedicated Slack or Microsoft Teams channels with daily asynchronous updates, weekly milestone demos, and direct WhatsApp escalation for urgent operational alerts.

What support is provided after the system goes live in production?

Every turnkey delivery includes a complimentary 30-day production warranty covering bug fixes, minor parameter adjustments, and operational stabilization. Beyond the warranty, ongoing coverage is available via a fractional advisory retainer or an SLA-backed maintenance agreement.
